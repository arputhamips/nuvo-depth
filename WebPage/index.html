SBC-BASED BY DESIGN

A depth platform you can configure.

Vision One pairs two wide-angle, hardware-synchronized global-shutter cameras with accessible single-board compute. Capture matched stereo frames, take control of the imaging pipeline, and keep the hardware within reach.

01 / CAPTURE

Global-shutter stereo capture.

Both color IMX296 sensors expose on a shared hardware trigger, avoiding rolling-shutter skew in moving scenes. Production units are planned to include built-in calibration profiles.

02 / PIPELINE

Control the image path.

Configure capture, rectification, stereo matching and output. Access associated image frames, disparity, depth, confidence and validity through developer interfaces.

03 / HARDWARE

Designed to be opened.

Vision One is intended for easy assembly and disassembly, with direct access to camera and compute components for integration and service.

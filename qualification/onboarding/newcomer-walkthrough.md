# First-time-user observation script and form

Recruit five people who have not used Nuvo Depth: two preinstalled, two own-Pi
full, one SDK integration. Supply supported working hardware, internet and trusted
setup material; flash the approved OS for own-Pi. Record the exact candidate and
public documentation revision. Do not use an agent simulation as a human trial.

Read only: "Using the guide, obtain your first fresh usable depth sample. Explain
its calibration status and save or consume one valid distance. Say what you are
trying and any problem you encounter." Observe without hints. Any intervention
counts, including recovery instructions beyond the published guide.

The assigned guide is Start Here -> bundled Pi, own Pi/browser, or SDK. The task
ends only when advancing device/run/capture identity, depth plus validity, a
finite positive valid distance, authority and saved output are observed. Ask
the participant to explain preview versus accepted calibration and next action.

Targets are <=15 minutes active for preinstalled, <=30 own-Pi full, <=15 SDK,
with no maintainer hints. Report total time separately. Keep failed sessions;
after a workflow fix, rerun with new participants. All five must pass. An agent
can execute documented commands as an additional trial but cannot fill these forms.

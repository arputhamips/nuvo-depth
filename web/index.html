Depth profiles

Presets change stereo matching only. Capture remains 728 × 544 and exposure is unchanged.

Quality · candidate

Full-grid HH with 96-pixel search and 5 × 5 smoothing. Historical Pi output was 2.84 depth frames/s; current throughput remains unqualified.

Balanced · candidate

Full-grid five-path SGBM with 96-pixel search and 3 × 3 smoothing. Measure latency and scene accuracy before choosing this profile.

Fast · candidate

Full-grid 3-way SGBM with a narrower 64-pixel search and no smoothing. Trades near range and streak rejection for lower cost; throughput is unqualified.

Wide search · candidate

Full-grid five-path SGBM with 128-pixel search and 3 × 3 smoothing. Offers closer nominal range at additional cost; throughput is unqualified.

Images and measurements

Images are processed ISP mono or colour, not sensor RAW/Bayer. Depth is float32 optical-axis Z in metres; accept pixels only when validity is 1 and depth is finite and positive. Disparity uses matching-grid pixels. Matcher support is binary (0 / 255), not a probability.

This browser: compressed preview

Preview is for viewing, not numeric measurement. Set the device-wide source, codec and rate on . This browser can request a lower rate; it cannot raise the device ceiling. The encoder runs while a preview consumer is connected.

Selections apply to this browser. Closing the page or hiding the tab releases its output demand. Other SDK and browser consumers keep their own selections.

How outputs relate and how to use them

Unrectified images come from the ISP. Rectification aligns the left/right views; the matcher produces disparity and the calibration converts it to depth. A side-by-side image contains left then right horizontally. Numeric depth and images can use different grids: always read each product's dimensions.

Products in a data frame share its capture identity. Preview and data can arrive at different rates: associate device UUID, run UUID, capture epoch and capture sequence, not arrival time. Read products by name; omitted products are absent, not zero-filled.

SDK starting point: client.frames(products=("depth", "validity"), cadence_hz=2, delivery="best_effort"). A compressed depth preview does not preserve float measurements.

Method and operator checklist
  • Use a rigid, flat, matte board with a white border at least one square wide.
  • Measure the physical square and marker sizes after printing; do not trust printer scaling.
  • Lock focus and exposure for the capture series. Fill all image regions with sharp, varied poses and distances.
  • The solver rejects duplicate/blurred/clipped views, removes high-error training outliers, and reports deterministic holdout error.
  • A geometric pass validates model fit and baseline scale. Factory release still requires known-distance depth verification across the advertised range.

Preservation and recovery

Use one complete supported cohort. Rerunning its installer preserves per-unit identity and calibration. An upgrade alone does not require recalibration. If installation was interrupted, inspect sudo dpkg --audit and the installer error before retrying. Do not mix individual package versions or assume a previous cohort is a tested downgrade.

Quality · candidate

Full-grid HH with 96-pixel search and 5 × 5 smoothing. Historical Pi output was 2.84 depth frames/s; current throughput remains unqualified.

Balanced · candidate

Full-grid five-path SGBM with 96-pixel search and 3 × 3 smoothing. Measure latency and scene accuracy before choosing this profile.

Fast · candidate

Full-grid 3-way SGBM with a narrower 64-pixel search and no smoothing. Trades near range and streak rejection for lower cost; throughput is unqualified.

Wide search · candidate

Full-grid five-path SGBM with 128-pixel search and 3 × 3 smoothing. Offers closer nominal range at additional cost; throughput is unqualified.

Method and operator checklist
            
  • Use a rigid, flat, matte board with a white border at least one square wide.
  • Measure the physical square and marker sizes after printing; do not trust printer scaling.
  • Lock focus and exposure for the capture series. Fill all image regions with sharp, varied poses and distances.
  • The solver rejects duplicate/blurred/clipped views, removes high-error training outliers, and reports deterministic holdout error.
  • A geometric pass validates model fit and baseline scale. Factory release still requires known-distance depth verification across the advertised range.

              How monitoring affects performance
              
Image previews run independently of stereo matching. Depth and disparity previews compute only at the requested preview rate. Select the products and rates you need; unused output stages stay idle. Raw delivery is available over WiFi, Ethernet, USB Ethernet, and on-device integrations within each connection's bandwidth budget.
